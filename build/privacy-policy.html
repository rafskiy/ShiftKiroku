Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 2024

ShiftKiroku ("we", "us", or "our") operates the ShiftKiroku web application (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our Service.

Information Collection and Use

We collect the following information:

  • Email address (for authentication and account management)
  • Work shift data you enter (for your own use)
  • Google Calendar access (only if you choose to export shifts; we do not access any other Google data)

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the Service
  • To allow you to log and manage your work shifts
  • To export your shifts to your Google Calendar (with your explicit permission)
  • We do not share your data with third parties

Google Calendar Access

If you choose to export your shifts to Google Calendar, we will request permission to add events to your calendar. We do not read, modify, or delete any other calendar data, and we do not access your Google account for any other purpose.

Data Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase. We take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at your@email.com.
